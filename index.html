02 OUR STORY

ABOUT THE MUSEUM

Where the past becomes present.

The Museo Iloilo was conceived by the Ministry of Tourism in the late 1960s to spur tourism development in the Western Visayas region.

It is currently being managed and administered by the Iloilo Cultural Research Foundation, Inc.
